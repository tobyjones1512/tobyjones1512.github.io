Headshots

Lifestyle

Natural, candid lifestyle photography for models and influencers: coffee-shop, street, seaside and at-home scenes with real movement and personality.

Editorial & Fashion

Magazine-style editorial and fashion photography with styling, bold posing and strong lighting. Perfect for a fashion model portfolio and agency submissions.

Questions, answered.

Who can book the Up-And-Coming Model Portfolio Pack?

Anyone aged 18 or over. No agency, experience or previous portfolio is needed. It is built for aspiring models, new faces, beginners, actors, influencers and creators who need a professional model portfolio.

What styles of photography are included?

Headshots, lifestyle, editorial and fashion, beauty, character and expressive, glamour and boudoir, and hands and product. Each genre is shot and edited so your model portfolio shows range.

What do I get at the end of the shoot?

A USB drive containing both the edited photos and the unedited raw photos, plus a professionally printed portfolio book. Extra copies of the printed book can be ordered.

Where do the shoots take place?

Based in Blackpool, Lancashire, with travel to Manchester available. Get in touch with your preferred location.

How much does a model portfolio shoot cost?

Pricing is on a per-case basis because every portfolio is different. Email hello@thecaffeinemediacompany.com with a few details and you will get a tailored quote.

Do I need modelling experience?

No. Up-and-coming, first-time and beginner models are exactly who this is for. Posing and direction are guided on the day.

Is the glamour and boudoir work explicit?

No. Glamour and boudoir images are tasteful, consensual and styled to your comfort level. You decide what is shot and what is shared, and every participant must be 18 or over.

Can I order extra printed books?

Yes. Extra copies of the printed portfolio book are available as an optional extra, handy for agencies, casting directors and photographers.

Will I get the raw photos?

Yes. The USB includes the raw files as well as the fully edited finals.

How do I book?

Email hello@thecaffeinemediacompany.com with your name, age, location (Blackpool or Manchester), the styles you want and any reference ideas.
