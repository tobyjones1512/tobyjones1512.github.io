Features FAQ

Your music, organised. Then made.

Tracks keeps your library in order and gives you a studio to record, edit, mix and play it back. One app for Mac and iPad.

In development. Tracks is not released yet.

From library to finished track.

Studio

A magnetic timeline in the style of Final Cut Pro, with per-clip mute, solo and volume lines. Record from the mic and the take lands on the timeline.

Lab

Load any audio file, shape it with an effects chain and see the waveform as it plays.

Scores & lyrics

A scrolling piano roll, plus a lyrics editor that exports LRC, ELRC, TTML, KAR and CD+G karaoke.

CDs & vinyl

Rip audio CDs, or capture a record and split it into tracks, filed by artist and album.

On-device assistant

Ask for edits in plain words. A local language model does the work on your Mac, with nothing sent away.

Questions, answered.

What is Tracks?

A music organiser and production app for Mac and iPad. It manages your library and also records, edits, mixes and plays back music.

Where is my music stored?

Projects are .tracks bundles on your own disk, holding the project file, media and render files. Nothing is uploaded.

Does the AI assistant need the internet?

No. Agentic editing runs on-device with a small local language model plus built-in rules.

Can I make karaoke and lyric files?

Yes. The lyrics editor saves LRC and exports word-level LRC, ELRC, TXT, TTML, KAR and CD+G with audio.

Can I digitise CDs and vinyl?

Yes. Rip audio CDs, or record a record and split it into tracks automatically.

Which devices does it run on?

Mac (macOS 15 or later) and iPad (iPadOS 18 or later).

When is it available?

Tracks is in development. There is no public release date yet.
