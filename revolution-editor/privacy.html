Privacy Policy

Last updated 5 October 2026

Overview

Revolution Editor is made by Toby Jones. It works privately: no account, and no personal details.

What we don't collect

We don't collect your name, email or anything else, and we don't run analytics or ads. Your discs, keys and projects stay on your Mac.

Other tools

The app can start your own copy of Dolphin, use the git on your Mac, and run coding agents you've installed (Claude Code, Codex, Gemini CLI, Ollama). Those tools have their own privacy practices. If you use an agent, your request and the project files it reads go to that agent's service under its own terms. Revolution Editor sends nothing itself.

Diagnostics

If you share analytics with developers in your Mac's Privacy settings, Apple may send us anonymised crash reports. You can turn this off at any time.

Changes

If this changes, we'll update this page.
