Mario Kart: Double Dash!!

Move courses between Double Dash and Mario Kart Wii.

Set up

In Settings > Games, link your Double Dash disc and turn on its tools.

Double Dash to Mario Kart Wii

Right-click a course archive and choose Port > To Mario Kart Wii. Pick a scale (around 1.18 matches Nintendo's own remake of Mario Circuit). You get a .szs with the model, sky, collision, minimap and course data. Rename it to the slot you want to replace.

Mario Kart Wii to Double Dash

Right-click a track .szs and choose To Double Dash…. Pick the slot to replace. The app writes both the single-player and multiplayer archives and repairs checkpoints Double Dash can't handle.

Viewing

.bmd/.bdl models and .btk/.bca animations open as read-only previews. Test with Hot Swap (Testing).
