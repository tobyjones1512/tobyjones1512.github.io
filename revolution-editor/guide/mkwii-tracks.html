Mario Kart Wii tracks

Edit course data, collision, minimap and sky, then preview the track in 3D. New to this? Follow the step-by-step custom track tutorial or battle arena tutorial.

What a track is

  • course_model.brres: what you see.
  • course.kcl: collision.
  • course.kmp: start points, paths, checkpoints, respawns, objects, cameras.
  • map_model.brres: the minimap.
  • vrcorn_model.brres: the sky.
  • itembox.brres: the item box model (copied from your disc).

Course data (KMP)

A top-down canvas with an inspector. Load the track model behind your points so you can place them on the real track.

  • A pen tool draws paths with evenly spaced points. Snap to ground drops points onto the collision.
  • Make Testable fills in anything missing so the track will load. Check Laps drives the CPU route three laps to confirm checkpoints work.
  • Intro Cameras… builds and plays the opening fly-over. Advanced shows every field as a spreadsheet.
  • Right-click a point for Add Point Before and After, Duplicate (objects, starts, respawns, areas and cameras), Move Earlier and Later, Snap to Ground and Delete. Right-click empty canvas to add any kind of point right there. ⌘D duplicates the selected object, start, respawn, area or camera.

Collision (KCL)

Import a model (tick Convert Z-up to Y-up for Blender), then set each surface type. Select a triangle to retag everything connected to it. Wireframe and culling views help find problems.

Minimap and sky

Create Map builds the minimap from your track: tick the textures that belong on it (the road) and pick a shade for each. The sky picker makes a sky from a 2:1 panorama, your own model, or a retail track on your linked disc.

Objects, budget and preview

  • If your track uses objects whose files are missing, a banner offers to copy them from your linked disc.
  • Shadows: right-click course_model.brres and choose Mario Kart Wii > Create Shadow Map.... Move the sun and change its softness and strength while a live 3D preview shows the shadows on your track. Your last settings are remembered; Reset goes back to the default sun.
  • Track Budget (in the model editor) compares your model with every retail track. Stay under the retail maximum.
  • Preview Track shows the whole track in 3D, including a driver's-eye view. Create Render exports an image.
