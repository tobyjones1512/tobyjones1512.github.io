Archives

Open the compressed archives most Wii and GameCube games are built from.

What they are

A .szs or .arc is a folder of files packed into one archive. Double-click one to open it, browse it like a folder, and double-click a file inside to edit it. Wiimm's .wbz and .wlz work too.

Editing

  • Add files, make folders, rename, delete and extract from the toolbar or right-click.
  • Leave Yaz0 Compress on to save a normal compressed file.
  • The Info button lists what files each kind of archive expects.

Check and scale

  • Verify Archive checks that a Mario Kart Wii track or NSMBW level has what it needs to load.
  • Scale Track… (right-click a track > Mario Kart Wii, or the archive toolbar) resizes the whole course, sky, minimap, collision and course data together.
