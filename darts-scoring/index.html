Features

Every way you play darts.

From a quick match at home to a full pub league season, Darts keeps score so you can focus on throwing.

Match Play

Challenge your friends or opponents to classic X01 matches - the format used in professional darts worldwide. Choose 301, 501 or a custom starting score, apply straight-out, double-out or master-out checkout rules, and compete across multiple legs and sets. Every throw is tracked and validated automatically.

Competitive Play

Host multi-player competitions with round robin or knockout formats. Manage your player roster and let the app handle the bracket logistics - perfect for pub leagues, work tournaments or organised events.

Timed Games

Race against the clock in fast-paced timed games, scoring as many points as possible before the buzzer. Ideal for warm-ups or casual play with friends.

Professional Statistics

Track your 3-dart average, 180s scored, checkout hits, best checkout, and legs and sets won - the same metrics the pros use to measure consistency.

Intuitive Scoring

Our digital dartboard keypad mimics real darts - simply tap the ring, multiplier and number to log each throw. Undo any mistake with a single tap. No fumbling with pencil and paper.

Smart Validation

It knows the rules of darts.

Darts enforces real match rules automatically, so no one has to referee.

1

Proper checkouts

Enforces double-out, straight-out or master-out finishing rules - whichever your match calls for.

2

No invalid busts

Automatically catches busts - going below zero or leaving exactly one - so every leg stays accurate.

3

Two-dart finish checks

Validates two-dart finishes for full professional compliance, keeping every checkout legitimate.
