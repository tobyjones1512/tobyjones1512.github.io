Privacy Policy

Last updated 8 September 2026

Overview

Bullseye is developed by Toby Jones. This policy explains what happens to your information when you use Bullseye on iPhone. In short: the app is designed to store your match data locally on your device, with no tracking, analytics, or data collection.

Information we don't collect

Bullseye does not require you to create an account, sign in, or provide personal details to use the app. We don't operate analytics systems, advertising networks, or tracking services. We don't sell or share your information - because we don't collect it.

Match data

Your player names, match, competition and timed game records, scores, legs, sets and statistics are stored entirely on your device. This data stays local and is not sent to us or to any external server. You own your data completely.

iCloud sync

If you have iCloud Drive enabled in your iPhone settings, Bullseye can sync your match data across your devices via Apple's iCloud service. This sync is handled entirely by Apple and governed by their privacy policy, not ours. You can disable iCloud sync for Bullseye at any time in Settings.

Diagnostics

If you choose to share analytics with app developers in your iPhone's Privacy settings, Apple may provide us with anonymised crash and performance reports to help us fix bugs. These reports don't identify you personally. You can turn this sharing off at any time in Settings.

Subscriptions

If you choose to subscribe to unlock the full app, that purchase is handled entirely by Apple through your App Store account. We don't receive or store your payment details. See Apple's Standard EULA for the terms that apply to subscriptions.

Children's privacy

Bullseye does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children. The app requires no account, login or personal data to use, so there is nothing for us to collect or hold.

Third-party services

Bullseye does not use third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking services. The only external services involved are Apple's (iCloud for optional sync, App Store for subscriptions, and diagnostic reports if enabled).

Changes to this policy

If how Bullseye handles data changes - for example, if we introduce optional cloud features or new functionality in a future update - we'll update this page before that change ships.

Contact

Questions about this policy or how Bullseye handles data can be sent to hello@thecaffeinemediacompany.com.
