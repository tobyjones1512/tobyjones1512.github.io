Privacy Policy

Last updated 21 November 2026

Overview

Reveal is developed by Toby Jones, trading as The Caffeine Media Company ("we"). We are the data controller for the information described here. Reveal is a dating app, so it handles personal and sensitive information. This policy explains what we collect, why, who we share it with, and how to delete it. Contact: hello@thecaffeinemediacompany.com.

Information you give us

Account: Sign in with Apple gives us a private Apple identifier. We do not receive your email address. Your Apple Account confirms you are 18 or over; Apple does not share your birthday with us.

Profile: first name, date of birth, sex, who you're interested in, town or city, interests, age range and distance preferences, and the body-type tags you choose for yourself and in others.

Photo: one head-to-toe photo taken in the app, stored as three crops (waist-down, neck-down and full).

Messages: the messages you send, and a phone number if you choose to share it in a chat.

Reports: who you report and why.

Sensitive information

Your sex, who you're interested in, your photo and your body-type tags can reveal information about your sex life, sexual orientation and body. We process them only to run the service you ask for (to match you and show you to matches), on the basis of your explicit consent, which you give by creating a profile. You can withdraw consent at any time by deleting your account.

Information collected automatically

Location: if you allow it, your approximate location (rounded to about 1 km) is used to fill in your town and to measure distance to matches. Others only ever see a rounded distance, never your location.

Notifications: a push token for your iPhone, so we can tell you about new lineups, matches and messages. Notifications never include message text.

Read receipts: whether you have opened a chat's latest messages, shown to the other person as "Read".

We do not use advertising identifiers, run ads, track you across other apps, or sell your information.

Contacts

If you turn on "People you know", Reveal reads phone numbers and email addresses from your contacts and turns each one into a short scrambled code (a hash) on your iPhone. Only those codes, and codes made the same way from the number and email you enter for yourself, are sent to us, and only to stop people who know each other from seeing each other. We never receive names or readable numbers. Turn the feature off to remove them.

Checks on your iPhone

Before your photo is uploaded, Reveal uses on-device machine learning to check your pose, that your face is visible, and that you're wearing underwear or swimwear and not nude. These checks run entirely on your iPhone. The live camera feed is never uploaded or stored.

Who can see what

Other people only see you if you appear in their lineup, and only the crop they've reached: waist-down, then neck-down, then your full photo with your name, age, town, interests and body-type tags. Photos are blanked in screenshots and screen recordings. Messages are only visible to the two people in the chat, and a first message is hidden until the recipient says yes.

Service providers

We use Vercel (hosting and photo storage) and Neon (database) to run Reveal, and Apple for Sign in with Apple and push notifications. They process data only on our instructions. Their servers may be outside the UK; where they are, transfers are protected by the UK International Data Transfer Addendum or equivalent safeguards. Photos are stored at unguessable web addresses that are only handed to people entitled to see them.

How long we keep it

We keep your information while your account exists. When you delete your account, your profile, photos, matches, messages, contacts codes and push tokens are erased immediately, and we revoke Reveal's access to your Apple ID. Reports other people made about you may be kept for up to 12 months to keep the service safe. Backups held by our providers roll off within 30 days.

Your rights

You can access, correct, export or delete your information, object to or restrict how we use it, and withdraw consent. Most of this can be done in the app (edit your profile, or Delete account). For anything else, email hello@thecaffeinemediacompany.com. If you're in the UK you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk); in the EEA, to your local data protection authority.

Stopping Sign in with Apple

If you stop using your Apple ID with Reveal in iOS Settings, or delete your Apple Account, Apple tells us and we delete your Reveal account in the same way.

Children

Reveal is for adults 18 and over only. Sign-in requires your Apple Account to confirm you're 18 or over, and the date of birth you give must also be 18 or over. If we learn that someone under 18 has an account, we delete it.

Changes

If we change how Reveal handles data, we'll update this page and, where required, the App Store privacy label and ask for your consent again before the change applies.
