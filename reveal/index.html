Attraction first. Conversation by consent.

Reveal is a dating app for iPhone that shows you three people at a time, revealed from the feet up. Let one go at the waist, another at the neck, and meet the last one head-to-toe.

Coming soon to iPhone. Adults 18+ only. Beta access by invitation: email us.

How it works.

  1. 1

    Take your photo

    The app talks you into position with the back camera and checks the photo on your iPhone before anything is uploaded.

  2. 2

    Waist-down

    Three people who match what you're looking for. Let one go.

  3. 3

    Neck-down

    Two left. Let one more go. They're gone for good.

  4. 4

    Head-to-toe

    Meet the one you kept, with their name, age and interests. Send one message: they see you head-to-toe and say yes before they read it.

Built for the privacy of bodies.

Your photo is the most personal thing in the app, so it's handled that way.

Shown only as far as people earn

Your photo is stored as three crops behind separate unguessable links. A person only ever receives the crop they have reached in their lineup.

No screenshots

Photos are blanked in screenshots, screen recordings and mirroring.

Checked on your iPhone

Face, pose and clothing checks run on your device. Nudity is never uploaded.

Messages by consent

First messages wait for a yes. Notifications never show what a message says.

Delete everything

One tap erases your photos, matches and messages, and disconnects your Apple ID.

Questions, answered.

Who can use Reveal?

Adults only. Your Apple Account has to confirm you're 18 or over before you can sign in, and everyone you see is 18 or over too.

Why a photo in underwear or swimwear?

Reveal is honest about attraction: people see your body before your face, then decide. Two-piece swimwear or underwear, with your belly and thighs visible, gives everyone the same fair look. Nudity is never allowed, and the app checks every photo on your iPhone before it is uploaded.

Can people screenshot my photo?

No. Reveal blanks photos in screenshots, screen recordings and screen mirroring.

Who sees my photo?

Only people Reveal matches you with, and only as far as they've got: waist-down first, then neck-down, then head-to-toe for the one person who keeps you to the end.

How many people do I see?

Three at a time. Start a new lineup whenever there are three new people who match what you're looking for, and Reveal lets you know when there are.

Will people I know see me?

Not if you let Reveal check your contacts. Numbers and emails are scrambled on your iPhone before anything is sent, and people who know each other never appear in each other's lineups.

How do I delete my account?

Open the You tab and tap Delete account. Your photos, matches and messages are erased straight away, and Reveal stops using your Apple ID.
