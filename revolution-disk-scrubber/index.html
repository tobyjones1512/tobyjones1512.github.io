Questions, answered.

What is Revolution Disk Scrubber?

A native macOS app for shrinking and modding Nintendo Wii ISOs, in the spirit of Wii Scrubber.

Do I need Wine or a Windows VM?

No. It is built natively for the Mac.

Will scrubbing break my game?

Scrubbing removes dummy padding data only, so game functionality is unaffected.

Can I replace files inside an ISO?

Yes, for example custom textures, models or fan translations.

Is it for pirated games?

No. It is for legally obtained backups of games you own only, and we do not condone piracy.
