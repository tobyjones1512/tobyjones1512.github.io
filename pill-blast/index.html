How to play

Simple rules, deep puzzles.

Two-part pill capsules fall down an 8-wide, 16-tall board. Line up colours to clear the board of viruses.

1

Match colours

Line up 4 or more of the same colour in a row or column to clear them from the board.

2

Destroy viruses

Any match that touches a virus destroys it. Clear every virus on the board to win the level.

3

Survive the world

You start each world with 3 lives. Clear all 10 levels - including a boss on level 10 - to advance.

Swipe to move Tap to rotate Hold to drop faster

Features

Worlds that never stop coming.

Every world is procedurally generated - there's no fixed ending, and the same seed always plays out the same way for everyone.

Infinite worlds

Worlds and levels are never hand-authored - new worlds keep unlocking forever, each a little harder, each introducing a new mechanic to master.

Boss Rush

An optional gauntlet that runs you through boss encounters back-to-back in one continuous attempt - no pausing, no retries.

Challenge Mode

50 fixed, hand-picked puzzles across 7 categories - every player gets the exact same board, so it's the true test of skill.

Custom Board editor

Design your own boards - viruses, evil viruses, laser viruses, blocks and all - on the same grid the real game uses, then play them back.

Game Center

Achievements and leaderboards, including one tracking total Challenge Mode completions.
