Privacy Policy

Last updated 3 September 2026

Overview

Pill Blast is developed by Toby Jones. This policy explains what happens to your information when you play Pill Blast on iPhone. In short: the game is designed to work without asking you to create an account or hand over personal information.

Information we don't collect

Pill Blast does not require you to sign up, log in, or provide your name, email address or any other personal details to play. We don't operate our own analytics or advertising systems, and we don't sell or share your information — because we don't collect it in the first place.

Game progress

Your worlds, levels, lives, coins and streaks are stored on your device so the game can pick up where you left off. This data stays local to your device and is not sent to us.

Game Center

Pill Blast integrates with Apple Game Center for achievements, leaderboards and friend challenges. If you sign in to Game Center, your gameplay activity (such as achievement progress and leaderboard scores) is shared with Apple's Game Center service and handled under Apple's own privacy policy, not ours. You can review or turn off Game Center at any time in your iPhone's Settings app.

Purchases

In-app purchases — the one-time "Unlock Full Game" purchase, extra lives, shop items and bundles — are processed entirely by Apple through the App Store. We never see or handle your payment details; that information is governed by Apple's privacy policy.

Diagnostics

If you choose to share analytics with app developers in your iPhone's Privacy settings, Apple may provide us with anonymised crash and performance reports to help us fix bugs. These reports don't identify you personally. You can turn this sharing off at any time in Settings.

Children's privacy

Pill Blast does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children. Because the game requires no account and no personal data to play, there is nothing of that kind for us to hold.

Changes to this policy

If anything about how Pill Blast handles data changes — for example, if we introduce cloud saves or analytics in a future update — we'll update this page and, where required, the App Store's privacy label before that change ships.

Contact

Questions about this policy or how Pill Blast handles data can be sent to hello@thecaffeinemediacompany.com.
