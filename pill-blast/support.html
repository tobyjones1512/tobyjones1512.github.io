Frequently asked questions

I lost my progress - can it be restored?

Progress is tied to your device. If you've reinstalled Pill Blast or switched devices and your worlds look reset, email us with your device model and roughly when you last played and we'll look into what's recoverable.

How do I restore a purchase (Unlock Full Game or a bundle)?

Purchases are tied to your Apple ID. Open Pill Blast's shop and look for the "Restore Purchases" option - if it doesn't reappear, make sure you're signed in with the same Apple ID used to buy it, then contact us and we'll help sort it out.

Why did I lose a life or get sent back to the start of a world?

Each world gives you 3 lives. Losing a level costs a life, and running out sends you back to the start of the world - or to a halfway house checkpoint once you've reached one partway through.

What are coins for, and how do I earn more?

Coins are earned by playing and through daily, weekly and monthly streaks. They can be spent in the shop on items like Bomb, Rainbow Boost and Vaccine, or on extra lives, instead of paying real money.

Is Pill Blast free?

Yes - World 1 is fully playable for free. "Unlock Full Game" is a single one-time purchase that opens every world and level forever; everything else in the shop is optional and can also be earned with coins.

Something looks broken or crashed

Email us a short description of what happened, your iPhone model, and the world/level you were on if relevant - screenshots help a lot too.
