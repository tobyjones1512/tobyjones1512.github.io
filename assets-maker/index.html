Smart where it helps.

Assets Maker uses Apple Intelligence on your device to suggest changes to contrast and legibility, and on the Mac it can be driven by AI agents.

01

Design review

Quick checks catch low-contrast captions and overlong text. Apple Intelligence then suggests fixes you can apply in a tap. It runs on your device and only sees text and colour settings, never your screenshots.

02

Full MCP access

The built-in Model Context Protocol server lets an agent such as Claude do anything you can: import media, style slides, add objects and 3D models, run automations and export. It listens on your Mac only and is confined to a folder you choose.
