# Maestro Pro buttons and icons

Every button and icon, with the image to show. In a reply, put the image before the button's name, like this: `![Undo](URL) Undo`.

- ![Go to Beginning](https://maestro-pro.app/assets/icons/backwardu2eendu2efill.png) **Go to Beginning**: jumps back to the start of the score. (Buttons & Icons: The playback bar)
- ![Previous Bar](https://maestro-pro.app/assets/icons/backwardu2efill.png) **Previous Bar**: moves back one bar. (Buttons & Icons: The playback bar)
- ![Play](https://maestro-pro.app/assets/icons/playu2efill.png) **Play**: starts the music and the page follows along. It becomes a Pause button while playing. (Buttons & Icons: The playback bar)
- ![Next Bar](https://maestro-pro.app/assets/icons/forwardu2efill.png) **Next Bar**: moves forward one bar. (Buttons & Icons: The playback bar)
- ![Metronome](https://maestro-pro.app/assets/icons/metronome.png) **Metronome**: switches the click on or off. (Buttons & Icons: The playback bar)
- ![Key](https://maestro-pro.app/assets/icons/tuningfork.png) **Key**: opens the key menu for a backing track or karaoke file, to move the music up or down in semitones. (Buttons & Icons: The playback bar)
- ![Down a Tone](https://maestro-pro.app/assets/icons/chevronu2edownu2e2.png) **Down a Tone**: in the key menu, lowers the music by two semitones. (Buttons & Icons: The playback bar)
- ![Up a Tone](https://maestro-pro.app/assets/icons/chevronu2eupu2e2.png) **Up a Tone**: in the key menu, raises the music by two semitones. (Buttons & Icons: The playback bar)
- ![Edit Score](https://maestro-pro.app/assets/icons/musicu2equarternoteu2e3.png) **Edit Score**: opens the score editor. (Buttons & Icons: The score toolbar)
- ![Annotate](https://maestro-pro.app/assets/icons/pencilu2etipu2ecropu2ecircle.png) **Annotate**: draw on the page with Apple Pencil. (Buttons & Icons: The score toolbar)
- ![Wait for Me](https://maestro-pro.app/assets/icons/earu2eandu2ewaveform.png) **Wait for Me**: the music waits until it hears you play the next note. (Buttons & Icons: The score toolbar)
- ![Record Myself](https://maestro-pro.app/assets/icons/recordu2ecircle.png) **Record Myself**: records you with the microphone so you can review the take. (Buttons & Icons: The score toolbar)
- ![Tuner](https://maestro-pro.app/assets/icons/tuningfork.png) **Tuner**: tells you how far in tune the note you play is. (Buttons & Icons: The score toolbar)
- ![Inspector](https://maestro-pro.app/assets/icons/slideru2ehorizontalu2e3.png) **Inspector**: opens the panel with this score's settings: key, tempo, backing tracks, staves and appearance. (Buttons & Icons: The score toolbar)
- ![Chord View](https://maestro-pro.app/assets/icons/squareu2egridu2e2x2.png) **Chord View**: shows the chords of the score as a grid. (Buttons & Icons: The score toolbar)
- ![Show Lyrics Instead of Music](https://maestro-pro.app/assets/icons/quoteu2ebubble.png) **Show Lyrics Instead of Music**: swaps the page for the words. (Buttons & Icons: The score toolbar)
- ![Play Together](https://maestro-pro.app/assets/icons/personu2e2u2ewaveu2e2.png) **Play Together**: plays in time with other devices. (Buttons & Icons: The score toolbar)
- ![Zoom In](https://maestro-pro.app/assets/icons/plusu2emagnifyingglass.png) **Zoom In**: makes the music bigger. (Buttons & Icons: The score toolbar)
- ![Zoom Out](https://maestro-pro.app/assets/icons/minusu2emagnifyingglass.png) **Zoom Out**: makes the music smaller. (Buttons & Icons: The score toolbar)
- ![Fit to Width](https://maestro-pro.app/assets/icons/arrowu2eupu2eleftu2eandu2earrowu2edownu2eright.png) **Fit to Width**: sizes the page to fill the screen width. (Buttons & Icons: The score toolbar)
- ![Follow Playback](https://maestro-pro.app/assets/icons/arrowu2edownu2edoc.png) **Follow Playback**: scrolls the page to keep the playing bar in view. (Buttons & Icons: The score toolbar)
- ![Dark Sheet Music](https://maestro-pro.app/assets/icons/moon.png) **Dark Sheet Music**: shows light notes on a dark page. (Buttons & Icons: The score toolbar)
- ![Turn Pages Like a Book](https://maestro-pro.app/assets/icons/booku2epages.png) **Turn Pages Like a Book**: pages turn with a curl instead of scrolling. (Buttons & Icons: The score toolbar)
- ![Highlight Notes](https://maestro-pro.app/assets/icons/musicu2enote.png) **Highlight Notes**: lights up each note as it sounds. (Buttons & Icons: The score toolbar)
- ![Highlight Bar](https://maestro-pro.app/assets/icons/rectangleu2einsetu2efilled.png) **Highlight Bar**: washes the playing bar with colour. (Buttons & Icons: The score toolbar)
- ![Moving Line](https://maestro-pro.app/assets/icons/arrowu2erightu2etou2eline.png) **Moving Line**: draws a line that travels along the music. (Buttons & Icons: The score toolbar)
- ![More](https://maestro-pro.app/assets/icons/ellipsisu2ecircle.png) **More**: the rest of the commands for this score. (Buttons & Icons: The score toolbar)
- ![Settings](https://maestro-pro.app/assets/icons/gearshape.png) **Settings**: appearance, playing, listening, accessibility and shortcuts. (Buttons & Icons: The library)
- ![Help](https://maestro-pro.app/assets/icons/questionmarku2ecircle.png) **Help**: opens this guide. (Buttons & Icons: The library)
- ![Add](https://maestro-pro.app/assets/icons/plus.png) **Add**: add a score, a set-list or a file. (Buttons & Icons: The library)
- ![Import Score](https://maestro-pro.app/assets/icons/squareu2eandu2earrowu2edown.png) **Import Score**: brings a PDF, MusicXML or other score into the library. (Buttons & Icons: The library)
- ![Scan Sheet Music](https://maestro-pro.app/assets/icons/camerau2eviewfinder.png) **Scan Sheet Music**: photographs a printed page and reads it. (Buttons & Icons: The library)
- ![New Blank Score](https://maestro-pro.app/assets/icons/docu2ebadgeu2eplus.png) **New Blank Score**: starts an empty score to write in. (Buttons & Icons: The library)
- ![Search Scores](https://maestro-pro.app/assets/icons/magnifyingglass.png) **Search Scores**: finds classical pieces and jazz tunes to download. (Buttons & Icons: The library)
- ![Scales & Arpeggios](https://maestro-pro.app/assets/icons/pianokeys.png) **Scales & Arpeggios**: makes a practice score for a scale or arpeggio in any key. (Buttons & Icons: The library)
- ![New Set-List](https://maestro-pro.app/assets/icons/musicu2enoteu2elist.png) **New Set-List**: makes a list of scores to play in order. (Buttons & Icons: The library)
- ![Tracks from GitHub](https://maestro-pro.app/assets/icons/shippingbox.png) **Tracks from GitHub**: downloads scores and tracks from a GitHub repository. (Buttons & Icons: The library)
- ![Print](https://maestro-pro.app/assets/icons/printer.png) **Print**: prints the score. (Buttons & Icons: The library)
- ![Export as PDF](https://maestro-pro.app/assets/icons/docu2erichtext.png) **Export as PDF**: saves the page as a PDF. (Buttons & Icons: The library)
- ![Export as MusicXML](https://maestro-pro.app/assets/icons/docu2etext.png) **Export as MusicXML**: saves the notes as a MusicXML file. (Buttons & Icons: The library)
- ![Export Video](https://maestro-pro.app/assets/icons/film.png) **Export Video**: makes a movie of the music following along. (Buttons & Icons: The library)
- ![Rename](https://maestro-pro.app/assets/icons/pencil.png) **Rename**: changes the score's name. (Buttons & Icons: The library)
- ![Duplicate](https://maestro-pro.app/assets/icons/plusu2esquareu2eonu2esquare.png) **Duplicate**: makes a copy of the score. (Buttons & Icons: The library)
- ![Delete from Library](https://maestro-pro.app/assets/icons/trash.png) **Delete from Library**: removes the score. (Buttons & Icons: The library)
- ![Listen](https://maestro-pro.app/assets/icons/playu2efill.png) **Listen**: plays the music from the chosen note or bar. It becomes a Stop button while playing. (Buttons & Icons: The editor toolbar)
- ![Help](https://maestro-pro.app/assets/icons/questionmarku2ecircle.png) **Help**: opens this guide at the editor pages. (Buttons & Icons: The editor toolbar)
- ![Undo](https://maestro-pro.app/assets/icons/arrowu2euturnu2ebackward.png) **Undo**: takes back the last change. (Buttons & Icons: The editor toolbar)
- ![Redo](https://maestro-pro.app/assets/icons/arrowu2euturnu2eforward.png) **Redo**: puts back a change you undid. (Buttons & Icons: The editor toolbar)
- ![Options](https://maestro-pro.app/assets/icons/ellipsisu2ecircle.png) **Options**: the sound used for listening, beats per bar, editing with the Pencil only, history and more. (Buttons & Icons: The editor toolbar)
- ![History](https://maestro-pro.app/assets/icons/clocku2earrowu2ecirclepath.png) **History**: saved versions and branches of the score. (Buttons & Icons: The editor toolbar)
- ![Deselect](https://maestro-pro.app/assets/icons/xmarku2ecircle.png) **Deselect**: clears whatever is chosen. It shows in the middle of the toolbar while something is chosen. (Buttons & Icons: The editor toolbar)
- ![Notes](https://maestro-pro.app/assets/icons/pencilu2etip.png) **Notes**: put in and move notes, and set how long they last. (Buttons & Icons: The editing modes)
- ![Rests](https://maestro-pro.app/assets/icons/pause.png) **Rests**: add, change and remove rests. (Buttons & Icons: The editing modes)
- ![Marks](https://maestro-pro.app/assets/icons/textformatu2eabc.png) **Marks**: accidentals, ties and articulations. (Buttons & Icons: The editing modes)
- ![Dynamics](https://maestro-pro.app/assets/icons/speakeru2ewaveu2e2.png) **Dynamics**: loud and soft, crescendo and diminuendo. (Buttons & Icons: The editing modes)
- ![Bars](https://maestro-pro.app/assets/icons/repeat.png) **Bars**: repeat signs, and adding or removing bars. (Buttons & Icons: The editing modes)
- ![Form](https://maestro-pro.app/assets/icons/arrowu2euturnu2eright.png) **Form**: first and second time bars, jumps and tempo. (Buttons & Icons: The editing modes)
- ![Medley](https://maestro-pro.app/assets/icons/docu2eonu2edoc.png) **Medley**: copy, cut, paste and insert bars, even from other scores. (Buttons & Icons: The editing modes)
- ![Erase](https://maestro-pro.app/assets/icons/eraser.png) **Erase**: rub out notes and rests. (Buttons & Icons: The editing modes)
- ![Draw](https://maestro-pro.app/assets/icons/scribbleu2evariable.png) **Draw**: write over the music with Apple Pencil. (Buttons & Icons: The editing modes)
- ![Close](https://maestro-pro.app/assets/icons/xmark.png) **Close**: the red button closes the editing window. (Buttons & Icons: The editing window: window buttons)
- ![Minimise](https://maestro-pro.app/assets/icons/minus.png) **Minimise**: the yellow button shrinks the window to its title bar. Double-tap the title bar to bring it back. (Buttons & Icons: The editing window: window buttons)
- ![Fit to Contents](https://maestro-pro.app/assets/icons/arrowu2eupu2eleftu2eandu2earrowu2edownu2eright.png) **Fit to Contents**: the green button sizes the window to show everything in it. (Buttons & Icons: The editing window: window buttons)
- ![Back](https://maestro-pro.app/assets/icons/chevronu2eleft.png) **Back**: goes back from a submenu to the list. (Buttons & Icons: The editing window: window buttons)
- ![Sharp](https://maestro-pro.app/assets/icons/mu3aE262.png) **Sharp**: puts a sharp on the chosen notes. (Buttons & Icons: The editing window: notes)
- ![Flat](https://maestro-pro.app/assets/icons/mu3aE260.png) **Flat**: puts a flat on the chosen notes. (Buttons & Icons: The editing window: notes)
- ![Natural](https://maestro-pro.app/assets/icons/mu3aE261.png) **Natural**: puts a natural on the chosen notes. (Buttons & Icons: The editing window: notes)
- ![Tie to the Next Note](https://maestro-pro.app/assets/icons/tu3aTie.png) **Tie to the Next Note**: joins a note to the next one of the same pitch. (Buttons & Icons: The editing window: notes)
- ![Dotted](https://maestro-pro.app/assets/icons/tu3au2022.png) **Dotted**: makes the note half as long again. (Buttons & Icons: The editing window: notes)
- ![Copy Notes](https://maestro-pro.app/assets/icons/docu2eonu2edoc.png) **Copy Notes**: copies the chosen notes. (Buttons & Icons: The editing window: notes)
- ![Cut Notes](https://maestro-pro.app/assets/icons/scissors.png) **Cut Notes**: copies the chosen notes and removes them. (Buttons & Icons: The editing window: notes)
- ![Paste Notes](https://maestro-pro.app/assets/icons/docu2eonu2eclipboard.png) **Paste Notes**: opens a choice. Overwrite writes the copied notes over the ones already there and removes them. Insert puts them into new bars after this one. (Buttons & Icons: The editing window: notes)
- ![Duplicate Notes](https://maestro-pro.app/assets/icons/plusu2esquareu2eonu2esquare.png) **Duplicate Notes**: opens a choice: Inline puts the copy straight after, On Stave Below or To Stave Above puts it on the next staff, and To New Stave makes a new staff for it. (Buttons & Icons: The editing window: notes)
- ![Turn into a Rest](https://maestro-pro.app/assets/icons/trash.png) **Turn into a Rest**: replaces the chosen notes with a rest. For a rest, it takes the rest off the page. (Buttons & Icons: The editing window: notes)

- ![Pitch](https://maestro-pro.app/assets/icons/arrowu2eupu2earrowu2edown.png) **Pitch**: three rows of buttons to move the chosen notes up or down by a Half Step, a Full Step or an Octave. (Buttons & Icons: The editing window: notes)

- ![Articulation](https://maestro-pro.app/assets/icons/chevronu2erightu2e2.png) **Articulation**: staccato, accent, tenuto, marcato, fermata and more. (Buttons & Icons: The editing window: notes)
