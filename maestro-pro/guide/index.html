    The guide.

    Every part of Maestro Pro, one topic to a page. New here? Start with Getting Started. The same text is in the app, under Help.

    Start here

    • Getting StartedMaestro Pro is a working tool for musicians: it reads your scores and follows them to the note, turns pages hands-free, rehearses with a backing track, a song from Apple Music, the built-in instrument or a metronome, and lets you write and revise the music itself.
    • Your LibraryEverything you import lives in the library and stays on the device. It is also kept in iCloud when iCloud Drive is on.
    • Playing a ScoreThe bar at the bottom controls playback; the toolbar and the Inspector change how it looks and sounds.

    Practise

    • Tuner & Wait for MeMaestro Pro can listen through the microphone: to tune your instrument, to train a steady note, and to wait for you to play a score note by note. Sound is analysed on the device and is never recorded or sent anywhere.
    • Record & ReviewRecord yourself playing a score, then review the take with the notes lit: green where you should have been, red where you actually were. It listens through the microphone, so it works with any instrument or your voice.
    • Scales & ArpeggiosPractice material made on demand.
    • AnnotatingMark up the page like a paper part.

    Sound and words

    • Backing Tracks & SoundA backing track is a recording that plays with the score. The built-in instrument can play the notes on the page too.
    • Lyrics & KaraokeLyrics scroll in time with the music like in Apple Music, and CD+Graphics karaoke files show their built-in pictures.

    Write and keep

    • Writing & Editing MusicThe score editor changes the notes in a MusicXML score. A PDF is first read by the app, and the editor works on a copy of what it read. Open it with Edit Score (the music-note-list button; in Simple mode it appears for scores you created).
    • Version History & GitHubSave versions of a score, try ideas on branches, merge them back, and keep it all on GitHub.
    • Share, Export & PrintGet a score, or a video of it, out of the app.

    Together

    • Play TogetherUp to eight devices running Maestro Pro can play in time with each other (Advanced mode).

    Settings and help

    • Buttons & IconsEvery button and icon in the app, with a picture of it and what it does. Each line starts with the icon, then its name, then what it does.
    • Keyboard ShortcutsOn a Mac nearly everything has a keyboard shortcut, shown in the Playback, Score and View menus. On an iPad with a keyboard, hold the Command key to see them. Every shortcut can be changed.
    • Settings & AccessibilitySettings has the choices that apply everywhere.
    • TroubleshootingIf something is not right, check here first.
