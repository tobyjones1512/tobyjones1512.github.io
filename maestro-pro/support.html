Before you write

The guide covers every feature, and Troubleshooting answers the common questions: no sound, a score that will not follow, a tuner that cannot hear you, notes the app read wrongly from a PDF.

What helps

Tell me which iPad or Mac you have, which version of iPadOS or macOS it runs, and what you were doing when it went wrong. If a score is the problem, say what kind of file it is (PDF, MusicXML, MIDI).
