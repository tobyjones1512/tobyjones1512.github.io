TestFlight Beta

Maestro Pro is nearly ready. Before it reaches the App Store it is being tested through TestFlight, Apple’s app for trying apps before release. Register your interest and you will get an invitation by email when a place opens.

Register interest for theTestFlight Beta

Everything, free, while you test

Testers get every feature of Maestro Pro at no cost for as long as they are testing, including everything in the Professional plan: the score editor, medleys, version history and GitHub sync, alongside highlighting, Play Together, recording and review, annotation and export. There is nothing to pay and no payment details to give.

What to expect from a beta

  • There will be bugs. Beta builds are unfinished. Things may break, look wrong, run slowly or crash.
  • Keep your own copies. Maestro Pro copies what you import, so your original files are safe, but keep a backup of anything you write or change in the app. A beta could lose work.
  • Things will change. Features may be changed, moved or taken out between builds, and settings may reset.
  • Builds expire. Each TestFlight build works for up to 90 days. New builds arrive through the TestFlight app, which can update them automatically.
  • Your feedback shapes it. Use Provide Feedback in Settings, or take a screenshot in the app to send feedback through TestFlight.

What you need

  • An iPad with iPadOS 17 or later, or a Mac with macOS 14 Sonoma or later. There is no iPhone version. Which devices that covers.
  • The free TestFlight app from the App Store, on the iPad or Mac you will test on.
  • An email address you can open on that device, for the invitation.

How it works

  1. Fill in the registration form.
  2. When a place opens, an invitation arrives by email from TestFlight.
  3. Open it on your iPad or Mac, accept it in TestFlight, and install Maestro Pro.

The registration form is hosted by JackPoll, so what you enter there is handled under their privacy policy. It is used only to send beta invitations.
