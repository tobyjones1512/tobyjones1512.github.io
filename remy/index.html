Remy is a digital cookbook for iPhone - save the recipes you love, discover new ones from trusted creators, and get ideas built around your own tastes and allergies. No more mental maths converting grams to cups or Celsius to gas mark.

Features

Everything a real cookbook does - smarter.

Remy keeps your own recipes, helps you find new ones, and does the fiddly conversions for you.

📖

Your library

Save recipes into your own collection, organised into categories like Starters, Mains and Desserts. Search finds what you need in seconds, and everything syncs across your devices via iCloud.

🔎

Discover

Browse recipes pulled from trusted creators - Jamie Oliver, Budget Bytes and more - filterable by category, ready to save into your own library.

🌍

World Cuisine

Explore dishes by region, from Caribbean jerk chicken and curry goat to cuisines from around the world - a shortcut when you want something different.

✨

Ideas

Tell Remy what you like, what you dislike, and any allergies to watch for, and it tailors suggestions around them.

🔄

Smart conversion

Switch any recipe between metric and imperial, and oven temperatures between Celsius, Fahrenheit and gas mark - plus a servings adjuster that scales ingredients automatically.
