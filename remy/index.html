Features

Everything a real cookbook does - smarter.

Remy keeps your own recipes, helps you find new ones, and does the fiddly conversions for you.

Your personal recipe vault

Save, edit and organise every dish you love in one clean space. Group your collection with Recipe Groups - from quick weeknight dinners to holiday feasts - so the right meal is always at your fingertips.

Smart unit & temp conversions

Switch between metric and imperial measurements (grams, ounces, US cups) or flip oven temperatures (°C, °F, Gas Mark) without leaving the recipe.

Nutrition & barcode scanning

Snap a barcode or a nutrition label with your camera to instantly log ingredients, analyse macros, and keep your health goals front and centre.

Diet tracking & home screen rings

Monitor daily calories and macro targets, and glance at your progress anytime with the Diet Rings widget on your Home and Lock Screens.

Discover

Browse recipes pulled from trusted creators - Jamie Oliver, Budget Bytes and more - filterable by category, ready to save into your own library.

World Cuisine

Explore dishes by region, from Caribbean jerk chicken and curry goat to cuisines from around the world - a shortcut when you want something different.

Endless culinary discovery

Tell Remy what you like, what you dislike, and any allergies to watch for, and it tailors suggestions around them - backed by integrations with Open Recipes DB for community favourites and OpenStove for smart-kitchen compatibility.

Apple Shortcuts automation

Add ingredients to your grocery list, launch your dinner prep timer, or log meals completely hands-free using custom Siri Shortcuts.

Built for everyone

Fully optimised with thoughtful accessibility features, including VoiceOver support, Dynamic Type, and high-contrast, kitchen-friendly modes.

iCloud sync

Keep your entire recipe library, custom tags and dietary stats synced across all your Apple devices, at no extra charge.

Pantry & expiry tracking

Scan or add items to a shared kitchen inventory and get reminders before things go off - formerly our standalone Best Before app, now built right into Remy.
