Remy is your recipe book in one crate, cheered on by a very opinionated chef's hat. Save the recipes you love, cook them step by step, and earn XP, streaks and stickers every time you do.

Meet Remy.

"You haven't made that shakshuka in 32 days, and I'm starting to take it personally."

Finish a recipe in Step by Step to earn XP and climb from Kitchen Porter to Culinary Legend. Cook once a week to stamp that week's crate and keep your streak alive, and take Remy's daily dare to try something you've never made. There are 100 stickers to collect, and new app icons unlock as you level up. Prefer it plain? Simple Mode hides the game and keeps your progress.

Everything a cookbook does, and then some.

Keep your own recipes, find new ones, and let Remy do the fiddly bits while you cook.

Your recipe crate

Import recipes from websites, photos, PDFs or the Share sheet, and keep them as photo cards with cook times at a glance. Sort them into Groups, from weeknight dinners to holiday feasts. Your library syncs across your devices with iCloud.

Step by Step

Big, clear steps one at a time, with one-tap timers that alert you even when Remy isn't open, food safety tips, and steps read aloud if you like. On wide screens the ingredients stay beside the steps.

Any recipe, your way

Scale servings, switch between metric, imperial and US cups, flip oven temperatures between °C, °F and Gas Mark, and spot allergens at a glance.

Discover

Hand-picked recipes from food sites like Jamie Oliver and Budget Bytes, filtered by category and added to your library in a tap.

World Cuisine

Explore dishes from 16 cuisines, from Italian and Indian to West African and Latin American.

Ideas

Tell Remy what you like, what you don't, and any allergies, and it suggests recipes around them.

Best Before

Scan barcodes to build a kitchen inventory, get a nudge before things go off, and add what's running out to your grocery list.

Widgets & shortcuts

Recipe Spotlight, Today's Meals, Quick Actions and Blood Glucose widgets, Control Center buttons, and Siri Shortcuts.

Make it yours

Pick from nine app icons, from the classic chef's hat to a frying pan and an oven, in seven themes including Hand-Drawn, Union Flag and Pride. Level up to unlock eight more, from a carrot to a trophy, and collect every sticker for the crown, plus gold versions of every icon.

Built for everyone

VoiceOver and Dynamic Type support, steps read aloud, and symbol and colour guidance that marks up ingredients, temperatures and times.

Health, built in.

Turn on Diet tracking and Remy becomes a food diary that works with Apple Health. Add Diabetes Management and the tab becomes Health.

  • Log meals, scan barcodes and nutrition labels, and track calories, macros, vitamins and minerals against daily goals.
  • Record blood glucose, insulin and Metformin, by hand or from a Bluetooth meter, with daily reminders that badge Remy's icon until you've logged it.
  • Readings and insulin are saved to Apple Health too, and readings from other apps and CGMs show up in Remy.
  • Filter your history, add notes, and export a PDF report for your doctor.
  • General advice from the NHS and Diabetes UK, matched to what you've logged and always linked to its source.

Nutrition figures for recipes are estimates, and Remy's advice is general information, not medical advice. Don't use them to work out insulin or other medicine doses, and speak to your doctor or diabetes care team before changing your diet or treatment.
