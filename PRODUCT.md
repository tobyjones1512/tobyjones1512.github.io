# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

- Prospective clients of Caffeine Media: brands, musicians, families and event organisers who need video edited, shot, written, restored or live-streamed. Many arrive with a specific job ("digitise grandad's cine reels", "stream a funeral for family abroad").
- App Store visitors and existing users of Toby Jones's iOS/macOS apps (Bullseye darts, DoP Toolkit, Pill Blast, Remy, Revolution Disk Scrubber, Snooker Scoring, Ultimate Player, iFart) who need a product page, support answers or the privacy policy. Apple review also reads the support and privacy pages.

## Product Purpose

The homepage gets a client to message or email Toby about a project. Each app microsite explains one app, links to the App Store, and hosts its support and privacy pages.

## Positioning

One filmmaker, Toby Jones in Blackpool, covers the whole pipeline from script to restored master, so a brief never passes between suppliers. Credits: New York Film Academy training, an original feature (*Vengeance*) listed on The Black List, and Sky News election-night work.

## Operating Context

- Static site on GitHub Pages at thecaffeinemediacompany.com. No build step, no framework.
- Contact by iMessage (`imessage://`) and email (hello@thecaffeinemediacompany.com).
- Google Analytics G-QCV7XEQ8QT, plus a cookie notice. Decision 2026-10-02: GA loads only after the first user interaction.
- `apple-app-site-association`, `app-ads.txt` and `remy/r/` (universal-link fallback) are functional and must not change.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Keep all copy and information, and the page hierarchy (every existing URL and section), exactly as they are.
- Targets: an isitvibecoded.com score of 10% or less, and PageSpeed 100 in all four categories on both mobile and desktop.
- Whole-site scope: the homepage, privacy, terms, 404, and the 8 app microsites (index/support/privacy each).
- App microsites share one house system, each with its own accent colour taken from its app icon.

## Brand Commitments

- Name: Caffeine Media. Logo assets: `assets/logo.png` (stacked lockup) and `assets/mark.png` (cup + filmstrip mark).
- Voice: plain, warm, British, first-person ("Let's get the kettle on.").

## Evidence on Hand

- The real credits, awards, training and toolkit on the homepage, taken from Toby's CV.
- Real App Store screenshots for each app under `<app>/assets/screenshots/`.
- No testimonials, client logos, prices for video work, or showreel footage. Do not fabricate any of these.

## Product Principles

1. A straight answer beats a sales pitch.
2. Real credits and real screenshots carry the proof; nothing invented.
3. Fast and plain on any phone.
