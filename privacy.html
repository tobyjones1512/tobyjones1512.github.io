Privacy Policy

Last updated 3 September 2026

Overview

Caffeine Media is the post-production and video services practice of Toby Jones, based in Blackpool, UK. This policy explains what happens to your information when you visit thecaffeinemediacompany.com or get in touch about a project. In short: this is a simple brochure site with no accounts, no forms and no advertising trackers.

Information you provide to us

The site has no sign-up, login or contact form - getting in touch means emailing or iMessaging hello@thecaffeinemediacompany.com directly. Whatever you choose to share in that message (such as your name, contact details and project information) is used only to respond to your enquiry and, if you go ahead, to deliver the work. It isn't sold, rented or shared with third parties for marketing.

Information collected automatically

This site doesn't run its own analytics, advertising pixels or cookies. It's hosted on GitHub Pages, which - like any web host - automatically logs standard technical data (such as IP address, browser type and pages requested) for security and reliability. That logging is handled by GitHub under its own privacy policy, not by us directly.

Fonts

Page text is displayed using Google Fonts, loaded from Google's servers. Loading a font this way means your browser makes a request to Google, which may log standard technical data such as your IP address. This is governed by Google's privacy policy, not ours.

Links to other sites

The Apps & Games section links out to the App Store listings for Pill Blast and Remy, and other parts of the site may link to client work or third-party platforms. Once you leave thecaffeinemediacompany.com, that site's own privacy policy applies - this policy only covers our own pages.

Your rights

If you've emailed or messaged us, you can ask at any time to see what we hold about you, have it corrected, or have it deleted - just get in touch using the address below. As a UK sole proprietorship, we handle any such request under UK data protection law.

Children's privacy

This site is a professional services site aimed at adults and businesses, and isn't directed at children. We don't knowingly collect information from children.

Changes to this policy

If anything about how this site or its services handles data changes - for example, adding analytics or a contact form in future - this page will be updated to reflect it.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to hello@thecaffeinemediacompany.com.
