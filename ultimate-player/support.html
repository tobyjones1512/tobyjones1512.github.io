Frequently asked questions

How do I connect to my Jellyfin or Plex server?

Open the app and tap "Add Server". Enter your server's address (local IP or remote URL), port number, and login credentials. Ultimate Player will attempt to connect and verify your server. If you have trouble with remote access, check that you've configured remote access in your server settings and that port forwarding is set up correctly on your router.

Does Ultimate Player support IPTV?

Yes. Ultimate Player supports IPTV/Xtream streaming directly. Add an IPTV source the same way you'd add a Jellyfin or Plex server - provide your IPTV provider's URL, port, and credentials.

Does my playback position sync across devices?

Yes. When you're signed into the same iCloud account across your devices, your playback position, watch history, and server connections sync automatically. This can take a minute or two; if it's not syncing, check that iCloud Drive is enabled for Ultimate Player in Settings.

How does 3D conversion work?

Our AI-powered 3D conversion analyzes 2D videos and generates depth information to create a 3D experience. Converted videos are processed and stored locally or on your server - we don't upload or store your content. 3D conversion uses credits (credit pricing available in the app).

Will 3D conversion work offline?

3D conversion requires an active internet connection to process on our servers. Once converted, your 3D videos are stored on your device or server and can be watched offline.

Can I watch on multiple servers at once?

Absolutely. Ultimate Player supports multiple server connections. Add as many servers as you'd like, and switch between them seamlessly from the app's server selector.

Is my content private?

Yes. All video streams between your devices and your servers are handled locally. We never access, store, or see your media files - that's the Ultimate Player promise.

Something looks broken or crashed

Email us a short description of what happened, your device model, and your iOS version - screenshots help a lot too. If a specific server or video file is causing issues, let us know so we can debug faster.
