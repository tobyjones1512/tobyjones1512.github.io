Ultimate Player streams your personal Jellyfin server, Plex library or IPTV source to iPhone, iPad, Mac, Apple TV and Apple Vision Pro, with native 3D and spatial video playback and AI-powered 2D-to-3D conversion built in.

Entertainment reimagined for every screen.

From 3D conversion to universal server support, here's what Ultimate Player handles for you.

Stunning 3D Video Playback

Watch native 3D videos and spatial videos in their full glory. Ultimate Player supports the latest spatial video formats, bringing immersive depth and dimension to your entertainment. Experience movies and content the way creators intended.

AI-Powered 3D Conversion

Transform any 2D video into captivating 3D with our advanced AI conversion technology. Watch your entire media library in 3D with crystal-clear rendering.

Universal Media Servers

Connect to Jellyfin, Plex, Emby, and more. Stream your personal video collection from your home network or from anywhere in the world.

Live TV & DVR

Never miss a show. Stream live television directly through IPTV/Xtream sources. Manage your favourite channels and catch up on recorded content anytime, anywhere.

Cross-Platform Sync

Start watching on your iPhone, finish on your Mac, Apple TV or Apple Vision Pro. Your playback position, preferences, and viewing history follow you across every device via iCloud integration.

Built for Apple Vision Pro
