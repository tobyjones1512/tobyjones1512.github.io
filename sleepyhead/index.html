Get up. Actually up.

Sleepyhead is an alarm you can't sleep through. There's no snooze button. To stop the alarm you finish a wake-up mission, like a sum, a memory pattern or a photo of your kettle. Keep it up and your streak grows, your pet thrives and your alarm creeps earlier.

Coming soon on theApp Store

Sleepyhead is a subscription with monthly and annual plans. Cancel any time in your Apple ID settings.

No snooze. No excuses.

You set the goal the night before. In the morning, Sleepyhead holds you to it.

Seven wake-up missions

When the alarm rings you get one at random: Maths Problems, Memory Sequence, Type It Out, Object Hunt, Scan a Code, Shake It Off or Walk It Off. Object Hunt sends you to photograph something like the kettle or a door. Scan a Code needs a barcode you stuck somewhere far from bed. Walk It Off counts real steps.

Rings like a real alarm

Built on Apple's AlarmKit, so it sounds through Silent mode and Focus, even when Sleepyhead isn't open.

Four difficulty levels

Easy is one quick round. Brutal is four rounds of harder sums, longer patterns and more steps.

No moving the goalpost

Within four hours of an alarm, its time locks. You can't push it back at 3am.

Streaks and freezes

Finish the mission within your streak window to keep the streak going. A streak freeze covers one missed morning when life happens.

A pet that's counting on you

Adopt a dog, cat, rabbit, guinea pig, fox or penguin. Wins earn coins for treats and accessories. Misses make your pet sad. It's optional.

Bedtime focus

Block the apps that keep you up, using Apple's Screen Time, and check in when you go to bed.

Wake-up insights

A 28-day calendar, your win rate, your typical solve time and which missions actually get you out of bed.

Stays on your iPhone

No account, no ads and no tracking. Camera and motion missions are checked on your iPhone and nothing is saved.

Wide awake in a minute.

Become a morning person, 15 minutes at a time.

Sunrise Quest moves your alarm from the time you wake up now to the time you want, in small steps you'll barely notice. It's hard on the habit, not on you.

01

Small steps

Choose a goal up to four hours earlier. Each step moves your alarm 15 minutes, and your bedtime follows to protect your sleep.

02

Best of three

Win two mornings and you move up a step. Miss two and you slip back one, but never back to the start.

03

Boss mornings

The morning that can clear a step brings a harder mission and a bigger reward. Beat it to move on.

04

XP, levels and rewards

Wins, quick solves, bedtime check-ins and the weekly challenge earn XP. As you level up, your pet grows from Baby to Legendary and you unlock new alarm tones and skies.
