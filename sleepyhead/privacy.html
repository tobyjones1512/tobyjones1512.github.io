Privacy Policy

Last updated 3 October 2026

Overview

Sleepyhead is developed by Toby Jones. Sleepyhead keeps everything on your iPhone. It has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking, and it doesn't connect to any server of ours.

What stays on your device

  • Your wake-up schedule, streaks, history, XP, Sunrise Quest progress, pet and settings are stored only on your iPhone.
  • Camera: frames for Object Hunt and Scan a Code missions are checked on your iPhone and are never saved or sent anywhere.
  • Motion: step and motion readings for Walk It Off and Shake It Off are read on your iPhone and never stored.
  • Bedtime focus: the apps you choose are handled by Apple's Screen Time frameworks. Sleepyhead can't see which apps they are.
  • Alarms and reminders are scheduled on your iPhone using AlarmKit and local notifications.

What we collect

Nothing.

Purchases

Subscriptions are processed by Apple. We never see your payment details. Manage or cancel in Settings › Apple ID › Subscriptions.

Deleting your data

Settings › Reset progress in the app clears your history. Deleting the app removes everything.
