Frequently asked questions

Where do the ratings come from?

Every rating comes from the authority that inspected the business. In the UK that's the Food Standards Agency's public data, filled in by local councils. In the US it's the open data published by the city or county health department (New York City, Chicago, Austin, Montgomery County MD and Boulder County CO). We don't change or add to it. If a rating looks wrong, contact the council or health department that inspected the business.

Why can't I see a place?

New businesses, and places waiting for their first inspection, may not have a rating yet. Some businesses are exempt. Try searching by name if it doesn't show in Nearby.

Why are ratings in Scotland different?

Scotland uses its own scheme, run by Food Standards Scotland, where businesses get Pass or Improvement Required instead of a 0 to 5 score.

Why does Nearby show nothing?

Nearby and the map need location access. In the Settings app, find Food Hygiene Ratings and allow location while using the app. You can still search without it.

A place has a rating of 0. Is it closed?

Not necessarily. 0 means urgent improvement is needed, and the council will be following it up. Businesses can ask for a re-inspection once they've fixed things.

Which places are supported?

The UK, New York City, Chicago, Austin (Texas), Montgomery County (Maryland) and Boulder County (Colorado). The app uses your location to pick the right scheme. In Settings, choose Where ratings are available for the full list. If you're somewhere else, the app tells you it isn't supported yet. We're adding more places over time.

How do I use the map and widgets?

The Map tab shows nearby places as pins coloured by rating. To add the widget, press and hold the Home Screen, tap the plus button and choose Nearby Ratings. Widgets need location access to be allowed in the app. They refresh about every 30 minutes.

How do I change the app icon?

Open the app, tap the settings gear and choose an icon under App Icon. Classic, UK flag and US flag are available.

What does Buy me a coffee do?

It's an optional tip in Settings (one, two or three coffees) to say thanks. It doesn't unlock anything. Tips are one-off purchases handled by Apple, and you can't restore them because there's nothing to restore.

Is this an official FSA app?

No. It uses the FSA's open data under the Open Government Licence, and open data from US city and county governments, but it isn't made by or affiliated with the Food Standards Agency or any health department.

Does it cost anything?

No. The app is free and supported by ads. You can remove ads with a one-off purchase, and you can leave an optional tip if you like it. Neither changes what the app can do.
