How clean is it, really?

Food businesses don't always put their hygiene score in the window. FSA Food Hygiene Ratings shows you the official one anyway, for hundreds of thousands of restaurants, cafes, takeaways, pubs and supermarkets across the UK, in Toronto and in a growing list of US cities, using each area's own rating system.

Official data, wherever you are

Every rating comes straight from the authority that inspected the place: the Food Standards Agency in the UK, and city, county and public health departments in Canada and the US. The app finds where you are and shows the right scheme.

Where it works.

Each place publishes its own scores, so the app uses each area's own system and wording.

UK

United Kingdom

Food Hygiene Rating Scheme, 0 to 5. Scotland uses Pass or Improvement Required.

TOR

Toronto, Canada

DineSafe: Pass, Conditional Pass or Closed. Downloaded to your phone about once a month.

NYC

New York City

Grade A, B or C from the Department of Health.

CHI

Chicago

Pass, Pass with Conditions or Fail.

ATX

Austin, Texas

A score out of 100. 90 and above is best, below 70 fails.

MD

Montgomery County, Maryland

Pass or Fail.

CO

Boulder County, Colorado

Pass, Reinspection Required or Closure.

The score, before the first bite.

UK ratings explained.

In England, Wales and Northern Ireland, councils score every food business from 0 to 5 on how it handles food, the state of the building and how it manages hygiene.

5

Very good

Hygiene standards are very good and fully comply with the law.

4

Good

Hygiene standards are good.

3

Generally satisfactory

Hygiene standards are generally satisfactory.

2

Some improvement necessary

Some things need to get better.

1

Major improvement necessary

A lot needs to get better.

0

Urgent improvement necessary

Things need to get better, now.
