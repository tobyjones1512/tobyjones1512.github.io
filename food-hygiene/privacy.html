Privacy Policy

Last updated 4 October 2026

Overview

FSA Food Hygiene Ratings is developed by Toby Jones. It has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking, and it doesn't connect to any server of ours.

Location

If you allow it, your location is used to find places near you. To do that, your approximate position is sent to the Food Standards Agency's public ratings service, along with any search you type. We never see or store it. You can say no and still search by name.

Other services

  • Food Standards Agency: rating data is fetched from the FSA's public API. See the FSA's own privacy notice for how they handle requests.
  • Apple Maps: maps and directions are provided by Apple.
  • Websites and calls: opening a business's website or calling it hands you over to Safari or the Phone app.

What we collect

Nothing.
