Privacy Policy

Last updated 4 October 2026

Overview

FSA Food Hygiene Ratings is developed by Toby Jones. It's free and supported by ads from Google AdMob. It has no accounts and no analytics of its own, and it doesn't connect to any server of ours.

Location

If you allow it, your location is used to find places near you. To do that, your approximate position is sent to the public ratings service for your area, along with any search you type: the Food Standards Agency in the UK, or, in the US, the open data portal of your city or county. In Toronto no location is sent: the app downloads the city's public DineSafe file and looks up places on your phone. We never see or store it. The Nearby Ratings widget uses your location in the same way when it refreshes, and only if you've allowed location in the app. To work out which country you're in, your position may also be checked against Apple's reverse geocoding service.

Other services

  • Food Standards Agency: UK rating data is fetched from the FSA's public API. See the FSA's own privacy notice for how they handle requests.
  • City of Toronto Open Data: about once a month the app downloads the public DineSafe file (about 46 MB) from the City of Toronto. The request doesn't include your location. Contains information licensed under the Open Government Licence - Toronto.
  • US open data portals: in supported US areas, rating data is fetched from NYC Open Data, the City of Chicago Data Portal, Austin Open Data, Montgomery County Open Data or the Colorado Information Marketplace. Each has its own privacy notice.
  • Apple: purchases (removing ads, and the optional Buy me a coffee tips) are handled by Apple. We never see your payment details. Tips unlock nothing.
  • Apple Maps: the map tab, maps and directions are provided by Apple.
  • Websites and calls: opening a business's website or calling it hands you over to Safari or the Phone app.

Advertising

FSA Food Hygiene Ratings shows ads from Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, Google's software on your iPhone collects some information and sends it to Google:

  • your IP address and approximate location derived from it
  • device details such as model, operating system and language
  • how you interact with ads (views and taps), and crash data from the ad software
  • your device's advertising identifier, only if you allow tracking

Your choices:

  • Allow tracking. The app asks once, using Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt. If you allow it, Google may use your advertising identifier to show ads that suit you better and to measure them across apps and websites. If you ask the app not to track, ads still show but aren't personalised that way. Change it any time in Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking.
  • Consent (UK and EU). Where the law requires it, the app shows Google's consent form before any ads load.

Google's use of this information is covered by Google's Privacy Policy and how Google uses information from apps that use its services.

What we collect

Nothing. We don't sell your information or share it with data brokers. Apart from the ads described above, nothing about you is shared with third parties for advertising.
