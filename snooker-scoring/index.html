Questions, answered.

What is Snooker Scoring?

Snooker Scoring is an iPhone app for keeping score and tracking matches: shots, frames, breaks, tournaments, statistics and a full match history.

Is Snooker Scoring free?

Yes. Every feature is free and supported by ads. An optional subscription removes the ads and can be cancelled any time in your App Store settings.

Can I run a tournament or league?

Yes. Tournament mode manages multi-match events with automatic scoring, player progression and final standings.

What statistics does it show?

Win rates, average breaks and consistency trends, broken down by match, frame and opponent.

Can I share or print results?

Yes. Generate PDF scorecards and tournament reports to print, share with opponents or archive.

Is my match history saved?

Every match is saved automatically, and you can search and filter by date, opponent or result.
