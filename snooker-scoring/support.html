Frequently asked questions

How do I get started with my first match?

Download the app from the App Store - it's completely free. Start a match, then simply tap to record each shot - the app handles the scoring automatically.

Is the app really free?

Yes. Every feature is free to use, including unlimited matches, tournament mode, statistics, match history and PDF exports. The app is supported by ads.

How do I remove the ads?

You can subscribe from within the app to remove all ads. The subscription is optional and can be managed or cancelled at any time in your App Store account settings.

Can I edit a match after I've finished recording it?

Yes. Open the match from your history, tap "Edit" and you can adjust frames, scores, and individual shot records. All changes are saved automatically.

How do I use tournament mode?

Create a new tournament from the home screen, add your players, and the app will organise matches and track standings automatically. You can record results for each match, and the tournament bracket or round-robin table updates in real time.

Can I export or share match records?

Yes - tap the three-dot menu on any match and select "Export as PDF" to generate a scorecard you can print, email or save. Tournament reports are also available as PDFs.

Where is my match history stored?

All your matches are stored locally on your iPhone. They sync across your devices via iCloud if you have iCloud enabled in Settings, and are completely private - we never see or collect this data.

Is there a way to back up my data?

Your matches are automatically backed up to iCloud if you have iCloud Drive enabled. You can also export individual matches as PDFs for archival. Contact us if you need help retrieving archived data.

Does the app work offline?

Yes - Snooker Scoring works completely offline. Record matches anytime, anywhere, and they'll be saved to your device. When you're connected to the internet, they'll sync to iCloud if enabled.

Something looks broken or crashed

Email us a short description of what happened, your iPhone model, what you were doing when it crashed, and any relevant match details. Screenshots help a lot too.
