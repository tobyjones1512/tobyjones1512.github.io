Lists are ordered only by price or distance. Fuel Prices doesn't promote, hide or re-rank any supplier, and doesn't alter prices, timestamps or other published details. Display options, such as showing pounds or rounding, change only how a price is written, and the station page always lists the exact published price.

Reporting an error

If a price or forecourt detail looks wrong, use Report a discrepancy at the foot of any list, in Settings or on a station page. It opens the government's form. We can't correct data ourselves.
