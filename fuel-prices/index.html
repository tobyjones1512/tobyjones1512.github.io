Official prices, not guesses

Every price comes from the statutory GOV.UK Fuel Finder feed, not from other drivers' reports. Each one shows how fresh it is, and prices are shown exactly as the forecourt published them.

Fair by design.

Fuel Prices has no favourites. These rules are built in, and some are required by the Fuel Finder data terms.

01

No supplier gets an edge

Lists are ordered only by price or distance. No brand is promoted, hidden or re-ranked.

02

Prices are never altered

Every price and timestamp is shown as published. Display options such as pounds or rounding never replace the exact price on the station page.

03

Spot a mistake? Report it

A Report a discrepancy link sits in Settings and on every list, taking you to the government's form.

04

Ads never touch a price

Ads sit apart from the listings and never cover or imitate a price.
