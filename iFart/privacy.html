Privacy Policy

Last updated 14 September 2026

Overview

iFart is developed by Toby Jones. This policy explains what happens to your information when you use iFart on iOS. In short: the app is designed to work completely privately, without requiring you to create an account or hand over personal information to us.

Information we don't collect

iFart does not require you to sign up, log in, or provide your name, email address, or any other personal details to use the app. We don't operate our own analytics or advertising systems, and we don't sell or share your information - because we don't collect it in the first place.

Diagnostics

If you choose to share analytics with app developers in your device's Privacy settings, Apple may provide us with anonymised crash and performance reports to help us fix bugs. These reports don't identify you personally. You can turn this sharing off at any time in your device Settings.

Changes to this policy

If anything about how iFart handles data changes, we'll update this page and the App Store's privacy label.

Contact

Questions about this policy or how iFart handles data can be sent to hello@thecaffeinemediacompany.com.
