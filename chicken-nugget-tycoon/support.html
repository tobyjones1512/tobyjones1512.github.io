Frequently asked questions

I lost my progress - can it be restored?

Progress is saved on your device, in the save slot you were using. Open the ☰ menu and choose Saves to check whether your factory is in another slot. If you deleted the app or switched device, your saves can only be recovered from an iCloud or device backup. Email us with your device model and roughly when you last played and we'll try to help.

How do I restore a purchase, such as Remove Ads?

Purchases belong to your Apple ID. Open the ☰ menu, go to Settings and tap Restore Purchases. Make sure you are signed in with the Apple ID you bought with. If it doesn't reappear, email us.

How do I remove the ads?

Open the Gold Shop from the ☰ menu. Remove Ads is the first item. It is a one-time purchase that removes banners, pop-ups, inline adverts and promotions. Optional reward ads stay available.

The 'Watch an ad' button says no ad is available.

Reward ads depend on your connection and on ad availability in your region. Check you're online and try again in a minute. If you declined tracking or ad consent the amount of ads can vary but the button still works when an ad is ready.

How do I change the tracking or ad consent choice?

Tracking: open iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking and switch Chicken Nugget Tycoon on or off. In the UK and EEA a privacy options form is also available from the game's Settings when your region requires it.

Why did my machines break down?

If you don't play for a day your streak is at risk, and after that machines start to break and income stops. Tap a broken machine to repair it with cash or Golden Nuggets, or use Repair everything. Streak Shields protect your streak automatically.

What are Golden Nuggets for?

Golden Nuggets are the premium currency. Spend them on hiring staff, repairing machines and boosts. You earn them from daily rewards, challenges and achievements, or buy more in the Gold Shop. They have no cash value.

How do I hire staff?

Open the shop panel and tap Hire Staff. The price is paid in Golden Nuggets and rises with each hire. Each staff member speeds up all machines by 5% and taps for you.

The weather or time of day looks wrong.

The factory uses your device clock and, if you allow it, your approximate location and Apple WeatherKit. Allow location access in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Location Services, or turn the real-weather sky off in the game's Settings.

I'm not getting reminders.

Open ☰ → Settings and tap Enable streak reminders, then make sure notifications are allowed for the game in iOS Settings → Notifications.

Is Chicken Nugget Tycoon free?

Yes, the whole game is free. In-app purchases and ads are optional. See the Pricing section on the game's page.

Something looks broken or crashed.

Email us a short description of what happened, your device model, your iOS version and a screenshot if you can. That makes it much quicker to fix.
