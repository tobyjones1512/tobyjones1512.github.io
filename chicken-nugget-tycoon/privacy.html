Privacy Policy

Last updated 20 November 2026

Overview

Chicken Nugget Tycoon is developed by Toby Jones, trading as The Caffeine Media Company. This policy explains what happens to your information when you play Chicken Nugget Tycoon on iPhone or iPad. In short: the game does not need an account, and we do not collect personal information ourselves. The game shows ads through Google AdMob, which has its own data practices described below.

Information we don't collect

You don't need to sign up, log in or give us your name, email address or any personal details to play. We don't run our own analytics, and we don't sell your information.

Game progress

Your factories, cash, Golden Nuggets, streaks, challenges and settings are saved on your device so the game can pick up where you left off. This data is stored locally and is not sent to us. It may be included in your own device or iCloud backups, as controlled by your Apple settings.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

Chicken Nugget Tycoon shows banner, app open, native (inline) and optional rewarded ads served by Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, Google and its partners may collect and use your device's advertising identifier (if you allow tracking), IP address, approximate location derived from your IP address, device and app information, and information about how you interact with ads. Ads may be personalised if you consent and non-personalised otherwise. You can remove all banners, pop-ups, inline ads and promotions with the one-time Remove Ads purchase; with ads removed we don't request them, although optional rewarded ads remain available if you choose to watch one.

Learn how Google uses data at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Consent and tracking choices

On first launch the game shows Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt, asking whether the app may track you across other companies' apps and websites. If you're in the UK or European Economic Area, Google's consent form (a Google-certified consent management platform) is shown first so you can choose how your data is used for advertising. You can change your tracking choice at any time in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking, and your consent choice from the game's Settings when your region requires it.

Cross-promotion

The game may show a link to another app by the same developer. Tapping it opens the App Store. We do not collect any information when you see or tap it.

Location and weather

If you allow it, the game uses your approximate location to show your real local weather in the factory. The location is sent to Apple's WeatherKit service to fetch the forecast, under Apple's privacy policy. We never receive, store or share it. You can decline location access, or turn the real-weather sky off in the game's Settings, and the game works normally.

Game Center

The game integrates with Apple Game Center for achievements and leaderboards. If you sign in to Game Center, your achievement progress and total earnings score are shared with Apple's Game Center service and handled under Apple's own privacy policy. You can manage Game Center in your device's Settings.

Purchases

In-app purchases, including Golden Nuggets, power-ups and Remove Ads, are processed entirely by Apple through the App Store. We never see or handle your payment details. Purchases are tied to your Apple ID and can be restored from the game's Settings.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, the game schedules reminders on your device for your daily reward, streak and full vault. They are created locally and no personal information is sent to us. You can turn them off in iOS Settings.

Translation

If your device language isn't English, the game translates its in-game text using Apple's on-device Translation framework (iOS 18 or later). The text translated is the game's own interface text, and processing happens on your device.

Diagnostics

If you choose to share analytics with app developers in your device's Privacy settings, Apple may provide us with anonymised crash and performance reports. Google's SDKs may also collect crash and performance data. These reports don't identify you personally. You can turn sharing off at any time in Settings.

Children's privacy

Chicken Nugget Tycoon is a general-audience game and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Ads are served by Google AdMob, which may use data as described above.

Your rights

Because we don't hold personal information about you, there is generally nothing for us to export or delete. To exercise rights with respect to data held by Google, Apple or other third parties, please contact them directly, or email us and we'll point you in the right direction.

Changes to this policy

If anything about how the game handles data changes, for example if we add cloud saves or analytics, we'll update this page and, where required, the App Store privacy label before the change ships.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to hello@thecaffeinemediacompany.com.
