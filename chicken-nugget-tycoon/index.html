Questions, answered.

What is Chicken Nugget Tycoon?

Chicken Nugget Tycoon is a free idle tycoon and clicker game for iPhone and iPad. You build an isometric chicken nugget factory, tap to cook, upgrade machines, hire staff and watch the cash roll in, even while the app is closed.

Is Chicken Nugget Tycoon free to play?

Yes. The whole game is free to play. It includes optional in-app purchases such as Golden Nuggets, power-ups and a one-time Remove Ads purchase, and it shows ads, including optional ads you can watch for bonus cash.

Does the game ever end?

No. It is an infinite idle game. There are 24 machine tiers, and the tiers keep scaling in cost and income, so there is always another machine, another franchise and another rank to reach.

How do I remove the ads?

Open the Gold Shop from the menu and buy Remove Ads. It is a single one-time purchase that removes banners, pop-ups, inline adverts and promotions for good. Optional reward ads stay available if you want a bonus. If you reinstall or switch device, use Restore Purchases in Settings.

How does hiring staff work?

Spend Golden Nuggets in the shop to hire staff. Each member of staff speeds up your machines by 5% and taps for you every few seconds, so more staff means faster machines and more automatic taps.

Does the weather in the game match the real weather?

Yes. The factory follows your local time of day and weather: sunrise and sunset, clouds, rain, fog, snow and thunderstorms. It uses your approximate location and Apple WeatherKit. You can decline location access and the game works normally, or turn the real-weather sky off in Settings.

What happens if I miss a day?

Daily streaks pay bigger rewards each day. If you skip a day your machines start to break down and income stops until you repair them. You can repair with cash or Golden Nuggets, or use a Streak Shield to protect your streak.

Is there a Mac or Android version?

Chicken Nugget Tycoon is made for iPhone and iPad. It also runs on Apple silicon Macs as an iPhone and iPad app. There is no Android version.

Is Chicken Nugget Tycoon available in other languages?

The game is written in English and translates its in-game text into your device language using Apple's on-device Translation (iOS 18 or later). The first time, iOS may ask you to download the language.

Can I have more than one save?

Yes. You can create, load and delete multiple factories, and autosave keeps each one safe.
