Privacy Policy

Last updated 4 October 2026

Overview

Revolution IDE is developed by Toby Jones. This policy explains what happens to your information when you use Revolution IDE on macOS. In short: the app is designed to work completely privately, without requiring you to create an account or hand over personal information to us.

Information we don't collect

Revolution IDE does not require you to sign up, log in, or provide your name, email address, or any other personal details. We don't operate our own analytics or advertising systems, and we don't sell or share your information, because we don't collect it in the first place. Your disc images, keys and projects stay on your Mac.

Third-party tools

Revolution IDE can launch your own installed copy of Dolphin, and use the git command already on your Mac to talk to remotes you configure. Those tools have their own privacy practices, and the app doesn't send them anything beyond what you ask it to.

Diagnostics

If you choose to share analytics with app developers in your device's Privacy settings, Apple may provide us with anonymised crash and performance reports to help us fix bugs. These reports don't identify you personally. You can turn this sharing off at any time in your device Settings.

Changes to this policy

If anything about how Revolution IDE handles data changes, we'll update this page.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to hello@thecaffeinemediacompany.com.
